GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can I download directly from all four social platforms?

The link checker currently recognizes Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube URLs. Direct downloads require a separately configured, authorized integration for each platform. This starter project does not scrape those sites or bypass their restrictions.

Can I remove a watermark?

This site does not remove creator watermarks or attribution. Use watermark-free source files only when you have the rights and permission to do so.

How do I get smaller files?

Choose a lower quality setting when converting. Smaller files generally trade some visual or audio detail for reduced storage and data use.

Are my uploads kept forever?

No. Temporary uploads and generated output files are automatically deleted by the server after their cleanup window. Do not upload confidential or sensitive files.
